# Logos

- `cure-logo.png` — the Cure Health Care mark (500 × 500, transparent PNG, full colour: blue cross `#1089e3`-family, green crescent and hand, dark-blue "CURE HEALTH CARE" lettering). Copied from the Cure web app (`frontend/src/assets/cure-logo.webp`) and patient app (`assets/brand/cure-logo.png`).
- Use it on the boot screen, the About screen and marketing. On dark grounds (`night`) place it on a `brand-disc` circle with at least 10 % padding; on `day` it may sit directly on `screen`.
- No vector source or single-ink version exists yet; do not redraw or recolour the mark. A device-specific "Cure Home" wordmark is typeset in `display` (Sora 600) next to the mark, never merged into it.
